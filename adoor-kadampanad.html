Diocese of Adoor-Kadampanad

The diocese was formed by the Kalpana (decree) of Catholicos Baselios Marthoma Didymus I, and has been counted among the faster-growing dioceses of the church since. Its parishes cover the Adoor and Kadampanad regions of central Kerala.
