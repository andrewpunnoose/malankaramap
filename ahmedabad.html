Diocese of Ahmedabad

Constituted in 2009, the Diocese of Ahmedabad serves the Orthodox faithful of Gujarat, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh from its complex at St. Mary's School, Naroda.

History

The Diocese of Ahmedabad was constituted on 2 March 2009 by Kalpana No. 93/2009 of Catholicos Baselios Marthoma Didymus I. In its initial phase it was administered by the Catholicos himself, assisted by H.G. Geevarghese Mar Coorilos, Metropolitan of the Diocese of Bombay. The diocese was formally inaugurated on 3 October 2009 by H.G. Dr. Mathews Mar Severios, Secretary of the Holy Episcopal Synod.

Its territory encompasses the states of Gujarat, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh, together with the overseas territories of Oman and Yemen, although the church in Yemen is now closed. The diocese presently operates from the diocesan complex on the premises of St. Mary's School, Naroda.
