History

The Diocese of Kottarakkara–Punalur was established on 15 August 2010 by the division of the Thiruvananthapuram and Kollam dioceses, and its administration was formally inaugurated on 23 January 2011. H.G. Yuhanon Mar Thevodoros was installed as first Metropolitan. It is headquartered at the Kottarakkara Kottappuram Seminary, Pulamon, and covers parts of Kollam district and the neighbouring Shenkottai area of Tamil Nadu.

The diocese includes many small parishes that have declined because of extensive conversion to Pentecostalism, some, notably Chempanaruvi and Ambanad, having fewer than five families. These parishes are now receiving renewed attention from the Church, with large festivals organised there.
