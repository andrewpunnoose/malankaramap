V. Rev. — The Cor-Episcopa Rank

117 Cor-Episcopi

Mostly married priests elevated to the honorary rank of Cor-Episcopa, listed by the diocese under which they serve.

A cor-episcopa in the crimson cape and embroidered vestments of his rank.

The Highest Rank

The senior-most Cor-Episcopa in the Church holds the distinct title of Reesh Cor-Episcopa and Malankara Malpan: V. Rev. Fr. Dr. Johns Abraham Konat.
