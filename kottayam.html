Diocese of Kottayam

Following the decisions of the Mulanthuruthy Synod, the Diocese of Kottayam was formed with about twenty churches from Kottayam and the surrounding area. Its first diocesan metropolitan was His Grace Kadavil Paulose Mar Athanasios.
