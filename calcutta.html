Diocese of Calcutta

The Diocese of Calcutta belongs to the church's broader diocesan structure that grew out of the 1876 Synod of Mulanthuruthy, though it was formally constituted as its own diocese more recently, in 1979. It serves the church's parishes across eastern India.
