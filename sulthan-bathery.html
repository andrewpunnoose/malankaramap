Diocese of Sulthan Bathery

The diocese was formed in 1986, organizing the church's parishes across Wayanad and Kannur districts together with several churches from the Nilgiris district of Tamil Nadu. It has served the church's parishes in Wayanad, Nilgiris and parts of Kannur since.
