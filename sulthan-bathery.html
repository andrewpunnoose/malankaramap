History

The Diocese of Sulthan Bathery was established in 1986 to serve the growing Orthodox community of northern Kerala and adjoining regions. Its parishes had formerly belonged to the Malabar Diocese, but shifting demographics and the expansion of congregations led the Holy Synod to divide it. It united the Orthodox churches of Wayanad in Kerala, the Nilgiris in Tamil Nadu and parts of Kannur district.

Initially it had no local Metropolitan and was governed directly by Catholicos Baselios Marthoma Mathews I, who appointed Fr. Mathai Nooranal as its first administrator. In 1991 H.G. Kuriakose Mar Clemis was consecrated and became first Diocesan Metropolitan, bringing structured leadership and development. He was succeeded in 2009 by H.G. Dr. Abraham Mar Epiphanios, and the diocese is now led by H.G. Geevarghese Mar Barnabas.

Today it oversees 48 parishes, including its spiritual centre, St. Mary's Orthodox Cathedral and Pilgrim Centre at Sulthan Bathery. Its headquarters is the Nirmalagiri Aramana at Poomala, 300 metres from Sulthan Bathery town off the Kozhikode–Mysore National Highway.
