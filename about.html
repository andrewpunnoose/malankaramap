Our Purpose

Faithful record, freely given

To record the Malankara Church faithfully — every place of worship, every shepherd, every chapter of her story — and to make that record freely available to anyone, in the language they pray in.

01

Document

Every church, chapel, cathedral, monastery and shrine is located, named and classified, then filed under its diocese.

02

Preserve

Histories, saints, martyrs and scholarship once scattered across memory and print are gathered, credited and kept.

03

Connect

A family in Houston, a student in Pune, a pilgrim in Kerala — anyone can find the nearest parish, trace a diocese, or learn the Church’s story.

The Record

Four collections, one record

What began as a map has grown into a reference library for the whole Church. Each collection is cross-linked with the others, so a parish leads to its diocese, a diocese to its shepherd, and a shepherd to the history behind him.

Standards

The principles we hold ourselves to

A

Accuracy first

Entries are checked against more than one source wherever possible, and corrected promptly when a reader or coordinator reports an error.

B

Fidelity to the Church

The record follows the Church’s own order, names and canons — including the 1934 Constitution — and describes dioceses, ranks and institutions as the Church itself does.

C

Scholarship, credited

Historical studies name their research and sources. Where an article rests on a historian’s work, it says so plainly.

D

Open to everyone

Free to use, readable in English and Malayalam, and designed to work as well on a phone in a parish hall as on a desktop in a study.

Method

How the record is kept

1

Gather

Coordinators and contributors collect names, locations and histories from parishes, dioceses and published sources.

2

Verify

Volunteer coordinators who know their diocese and zone first-hand review each entry before it is filed.

3

Map

Each location is placed on the map and filed by diocese and by type — church, chapel, monastery, shrine and more.

4

Correct

Readers can report a missing or incorrect location at any time. Changes are reviewed and published as they are confirmed.

A note on the countsCounts reflect Orthodox-held institutions only. Institutions under schismatic occupation, and those founded since 1900 that have since closed, are listed on each institution and diocese page but not included in the totals.
People

Built by volunteers, for the whole Church

Malankara Map was created and is supervised globally by Andrew Punnoose, with a team of 100+ volunteer coordinators serving every diocese and zone — young people who give their time to keep their home churches, and the Church at large, accurately recorded.
