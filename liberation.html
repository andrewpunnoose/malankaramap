Liberation
of Churches.

Since the Supreme Court’s verdict of 3 July 2017, churches that were held from the Malankara Church for decades have been handed back to its believers.

The 2017 verdict declared the 1934 Constitution binding on every Malankara parish and the Malankara Church one indivisible trust. These are the parishes that have since returned, listed by diocese. Believers who were shut out for decades worship in them again, and the Church’s door remains open to every parishioner who will worship under the Constitution and the parish’s canonical priest. Parishes whose cases are still in progress are listed separately below.

Before the churches came back

For years, Malankara Orthodox families in these parishes could not enter their own churches. In some places the Patriarchal side went further. At Perumbavoor Suloko, a Patriarchal vicar issued a notice excommunicating seven Orthodox families and ordering that nobody keep any relationship with them or attend any ceremony in their homes.

The same hostility has been heard from the pulpit and the platform. In speeches, Patriarchal priests in Ernakulam have urged their people to cut Malankara Orthodox believers off, calling them “Methran Kakshis,” the Metropolitan’s partisans: not to cooperate with them, not to welcome them into their homes, not to support their businesses, and not to give daughters in marriage to their sons.

One Patriarchal priest from the Kandanad diocese went further still. Even at the point of dying of thirst, he told them, they must never give water to a Malankara Orthodox believer, nor take water or food from one, for the Orthodox, he said, are the people of Satan. If a brother, sister or parent should join the Orthodox, they were never to speak to that relative again, and they were to keep far away from the Orthodox altogether.

Parishes Awaiting Liberation

Proceedings concerning the parishes below, in which the 2017 verdict has yet to be given effect, are at an advanced stage before the courts and the competent authorities. None of them has yet been returned to the Malankara Church. They are therefore listed apart from the churches above and are not included in the total of 53, and no date of handover is implied. The Church awaits the lawful execution of the orders in each case.
