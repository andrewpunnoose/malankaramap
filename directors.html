The Directors

Malankara Map is researched, verified, and kept up to date by a global team of volunteers — an executive council, diocesan and zonal coordinators, and a handful of contributors spread from Thiruvananthapuram to Kochi, Brussels to Tokyo.

Leadership

The Executive Council

The seven who guide the project as a whole — its Global Director, Assistant Global Director, Co-Global Secretaries, Assistant Global Secretary, and Spiritual Advisors.

Verified
Global Director

Andrew Punnoose

Andrew is a student living in Hoffman Estates, Illinois. He is from the Mullamangalathu Vengalil family, a branch of the Aruveedan Valiyaveettil family of Kallooppara and has roots in Maramon, Karackal and Mavelikara. Baptized by Very Rev. Geevarghese Ramban (episcopal candidate), he serves his home parish as an acolyte. Andrew founded Malankara Map and continues to lead the project worldwide as its Global Director, coordinating the diocesan and zonal coordinators who help keep this directory current.

Andrew Varughese

Spiritual Advisor

Rev. Fr. Dr. Ninan V. George

Rev. Fr. Dr. Ninan V. George holds an M.Sc., M.Ed., B.D., and Master of Divinity (M.Div.), along with a Doctor of Ministry (D.Min.) from the Lutheran School of Theology at Chicago (LSTC). He lives in Pandalam and is married with two children, including Rev. Fr. Gregory Ninan. Spiritual Advisor

Rev. Fr. Jibin Philip

Rev. Fr. Jibin Philip was born and raised in Mundappally, near Adoor in Pathanamthitta. After completing his B.Com, he became actively involved in church life, and it was then that he met Zacharias Mar Theophilos Thirumeni, who brought him to Thadagam Ashram, Coimbatore, in 2013 to become a priest of the Malabar Diocese. Though he did not take to the place at first and returned home, a week of unease and the counsel of another drew him back, and he has never since wished to leave. By God’s grace, he continues at the Ashram as a priest and as Administrative Officer of Bishop Walsh Memorial Hospital, and he looks back on his life as a testimony to God’s grace.
