The Directors

Malankara Map is researched, verified, and kept up to date by a global team of volunteers — an executive council, diocesan and zonal coordinators, and a handful of contributors spread from Trivandrum to Kochi, Sydney to Chicago.

Leadership

The Executive Council

The three who guide the project as a whole — its Global Director, Assistant Global Director, and Global Secretary.

Verified
Global Director

Andrew Punnoose

Andrew is an eleventh-grade student in the suburbs of Chicago, Illinois, where he attends the International Baccalaureate Academy at Elgin High School. His family has roots in Maramon, Karackal (Thiruvalla), and Mavelikara. Baptized by Rev. Fr. Shibu Venad Mathai (an episcopal candidate), he serves his home parish as an acolyte. Andrew founded Malankara Map and continues to lead the project worldwide as its Global Director, coordinating the diocesan and zonal coordinators who help keep this directory current.

Assistant Global Director

Andrew Varughese

Andrew is a ninth-grade student in Warrington, Pennsylvania. Baptized in 2011 by H.G. Dr. Gabriel Mar Gregorios, he now serves as an acolyte at Bensalem St. Gregorios Orthodox Church and is an active member of the MGOCSM and other youth ministries. As Assistant Global Director, he helps coordinate the team behind Malankara Map — and the site’s background music is his own contribution to the project.

Verified
Global Secretary & Delhi Diocesan Coordinator

Rinzu Rajan Mathews

Rinzu spent most of her life in Delhi before moving to Calgary, Canada, where she lived until 2023; she now makes her home in Kochi, India. A biomedical researcher turned patent lawyer, she recently completed a four-month course in Orthodox Christian Nurture run by the Calcutta Diocese and is preparing to enroll in the Church’s two-year Divyabodhanam programme. An author in her own right, she regularly writes poetry and short stories, and serves as both Global Secretary and Delhi Diocesan Coordinator.
