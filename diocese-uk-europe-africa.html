Diocese of UK-Europe-Africa

Established in 2009, the Diocese of UK, Europe & Africa unites the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church's parishes and congregations across the United Kingdom, continental Europe, and Africa under a single ecclesiastical jurisdiction. As of 2026, the diocese comprises approximately 3,250 families, served by 59 priests with dedicated places of worship in the United Kingdom, Ireland, and South Africa.
