Church History page

Every judgment, covenant, letter and record cited on the Church History page, grouped by kind. The research and documents behind that page are by David George (malankarahistory.com).

Cited on Church History

Court judgments & legal records

  • Royal Court of Final Appeal, Travancore. Judgment of 12 July 1889, on the Malankara Metropolitan and the Patriarch’s authority.
  • Judgments in the Church litigation: trial court, 1919; High Court of Travancore, 1923; Full Bench, 1928.
  • The Patriarchal faction’s suit at Kottayam, 1938, and the list of churches filed with it as case assets.
  • Court ruling setting aside the “Acts” of the alleged 1903 synod.
  • Supreme Court of India. Judgment of 12 September 1958, and the dismissal of the review petition.
  • High Court ruling, 1990.
  • Supreme Court of India. Judgment of 1995.
  • Supreme Court of India. K. S. Varghese & Ors. v. St. Peter’s & Paul’s Syrian Orthodox Church & Ors., 3 July 2017.

Assemblies, covenants & the Constitution

  • Covenant of Maphrian Shukrallah with Mar Thoma V, 1754.
  • Manuscript colophon recording a priest ordained by Patriarch Gregorios with the consent of Mar Thoma.
  • Arthat Padiyola, 1806. The plate was rediscovered and first published in 1968.
  • Mavelikara Padiyola, 1836.
  • Encyclical of Mar Dionysius IV, 1843.
  • Parumala Synod, 1873 (founding of the Malankara Association).
  • Mulanthuruthy Synod, 1876, and its resolutions.
  • Managing Committee, Kottayam. Ruling of 1892 on the Patriarch’s attempt to depose Mar Dionysius V.
  • Association assembly, Kottayam, 25–27 November 1909, including the speeches of E. J. John and M. P. Varkey.
  • Constitution of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church, 1934, including Article 99.

Patriarchal letters & kalpanas

  • Excommunications of Mathews Mar Athanasius by Patriarchs Elias II, Yakub II and Peter IV.
  • Kalpana of Patriarch Yakub II, 1847.
  • Kalpana of Patriarch Peter IV, 1873, and his general kalpana from Cochin.
  • Kalpana of the Patriarch, 1910, on spiritual and temporal authority.
  • Letter of Alvares Mar Julius and Murimattathil Mar Ivanios to St. Dionysius of Vattasseril, June 1911.
  • Letter of Patriarch Abded Mshiho II, 17 August 1911, declaring the excommunication void.
  • Kalpana of Patriarch Abded Mshiho II, 17 September 1912, on the consecration of Mar Ivanios as Catholicos.
  • Letters of Patriarch Yakub III: 9 December 1958, accepting the Catholicos; June 1970, to Catholicos Augen I. Also Yakub III’s own history of the Church.
  • The Catholicos’s acceptance of the Patriarch under the Constitution, 16 December 1958, and the signed submission of the other side’s bishops.
  • Letter of Patriarch Zakka I to the metropolitans of the Patriarchal faction, 1998 (No. F30/99).

Contemporary records, 1905–1913

  • Malankara Edavaka Pathrika, 1905–06. Report that the Sultan had dismissed Abded Mshiho II.
  • British Vice-Consul at Diyarbakır. Report of December 1905 on the dismissal of Abded Mshiho II.
  • Account of the 1906 visit of two Malankara deacons to Patriarch Abded Mshiho II.
  • Konat Mathen Malpan. Writings of 1908 and 1910 on Patriarch Abded Mshiho II.
  • Za‘farān Monastery. List of its twenty-one councils.
  • Writings of the bishops of Abded Aloho and of the Mardin Board, 1912–13, on the state of the community.

Early Church, canon law & manuscripts

  • Didascalia Apostolorum.
  • St. Cyril of Alexandria. On John 20 and the Spirit given to the apostles.
  • Bar Hebraeus. On St. Thomas as the first chief priest of the East; and the Hudaya Canon (Nomocanon), including the 1898 Paris edition.
  • John of Ephesus and Michael the Syrian, on the Maphrian of Tagrit as Catholicos.
  • Maphrian Denha I of Tagrit, seventh century, on the Throne of the East.
  • Maphrian Basil Pilate, 1581, and his title “Servant of the Throne of St. Thomas the Apostle.”
  • Kodungallur manuscript, thirteenth century, naming the head of the Holy See of the Apostle Mar Thoma; and the use of that title by Mar Philoxenos II and Mar Dionysius IV.
  • Canons of the Councils of Nicaea (canon 6) and Constantinople (canon 2).
  • Neilos Doxapatres. Text of 1043.
  • Bar Salibi’s list of the Indians and the Syrians; and the Severus manuscript.

Scholarship & eyewitness accounts

  • Dinno, Khalid. On the patriarchate of Abded Aloho.
  • Hage, Wolfgang. On the jurisdiction of the Maphrian of Tagrit.
  • Fr. V. C. Samuel. Account as court interpreter in the Church litigation, comparing the Kerala copies of the Nomocanon with authentic manuscripts.
  • Konat Abraham II. The oldest Nomocanon manuscript, from the family library at Pampakuda.

Modern records & reports

  • The Oriental Orthodox Churches: Addis Ababa Conference, 1965.
  • Kerala State Commission for Protection of Child Rights. Case on the Kothamangalam gathering of 27 October 2019.
  • Government report on funerals held in Orthodox churches after the Supreme Court order, 2019 (32 Jacobite funerals).
  • Statement of Fr. Johns Abraham Konat, then Church spokesperson, on the funeral allegations.
