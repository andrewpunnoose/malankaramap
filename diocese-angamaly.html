Diocese of Angamaly

The Diocese of Angamaly is one of the seven original dioceses established at the Mulanthuruthy Synod of 1876, when Patriarch Ignatius Peter III of Antioch divided the Malankara Church's single metropolitan see into separate dioceses under their own bishops.
