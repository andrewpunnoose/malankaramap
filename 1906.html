Founding · 1877

The Veliyanad Synod

At the Veliyanad Synod (held at St. Stephen’s Knanaya Syrian Church) in 1877, Patriarch H.H. Ignatius Peter III of Antioch proposed reorganising the Malankara Church into seven dioceses, each under its own metropolitan. Though the proposal initially faced considerable opposition—including from Malankara Metropolitan Mar Dionysius V—and the synod concluded without agreement, the seven-diocese structure was ultimately adopted and became the Church's enduring administrative framework.

A common misconception holds that the seven-diocese idea was first proposed at the earlier Mulanthuruthy Synod of 1876. That synod, significant as it was, did not itself put forward the proposal — it was only the following year, at Veliyanad in 1877, that the idea was actually introduced.

The 1906 edition preserves this formative chapter in the Church's history.
