Founding · 1877

The Veliyanad Yogam

At the Veliyanad Yogam in 1877, Patriarch H.H. Ignatius Peter III of Antioch proposed reorganizing the Malankara Church into seven dioceses, each under its own bishop. Though the proposal initially faced considerable opposition—including from Malankara Metropolitan Mar Dionysius V—and the yogam concluded without agreement, the seven-diocese structure was ultimately adopted and became the Church's enduring administrative framework.

The 1906 edition preserves this formative chapter in the Church's history.
